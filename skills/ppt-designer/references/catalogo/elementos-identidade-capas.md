# Identidade, capas e divisores (21)

Sistemas de cor, capas, fechamentos e outros.

Ordem: qualidade, depois deck. ★★★ é a referência a copiar. Copie a técnica, nunca o conteúdo nem as cores do deck de origem.

## ★★★ Skim-layer lede
- **Origem:** AB InBev, p. 6, 7, 13, 15, 20, 28, 38, 43, 50, 57
- **Recorte:** `crops/e_b1_ab_lede.jpg`
- **Peça pronta:** `pecas_elementos_b.py::skim_lede`
- **Função:** Lets a judge read only the coloured words and still get the slide's claim.
- **Serve para:** Every content slide; replaces a separate 'key takeaway' box.
- **Como montar:** Two-line full-width grey sentence with a thick dark tick at left; 5-8 phrases set in bold accent colour, which is the only strong colour in the sentence.

## ★★★ Brand takeover: palette follows the protagonist
- **Origem:** CSN - ArcelorMittal, p. 14, 24
- **Recorte:** `crops/e_b4_brand_takeover.jpg`
- **Peça pronta:** `pecas_elementos_b.py::brand_takeover`
- **Função:** The colour instantly tells whose story is on screen, and a competitor slide reads as foreign territory.
- **Serve para:** Two-company deals, holding and subsidiary, competitor deep dive
- **Como montar:** Swap the whole theme token set by subject: CSN blue to ArcelorMittal red (also Cosan green to Compass red with corner logo swap). Nubank does it for a single slide: rail, accents, pills and corner logo turn Inter orange (p17), MELI logo on p54. Composite crop shows both.

## ★★★ Closing verdict with four numbered cards
- **Origem:** Restaurant Brands International, p. 49
- **Recorte:** `crops/e_b3_closing_verdict_cards.jpg`
- **Peça pronta:** `pecas_moldes.py::closing_verdict`
- **Função:** Ends on one giant word and compresses the whole deck into three theses plus a risk column.
- **Serve para:** Final slide before Q&A.
- **Precisa de material real:** peça ao usuário; nunca invente.
- **Como montar:** Cover photo at top and bottom; translucent dark red band across the middle; BUY! in huge white type; four white rounded cards straddling the band's lower edge, each with a square red tag at its top-left corner hanging off the card (I, II, III, !); red hollow diamond bullets; the risk card title is italic.

## ★★★ Dark mode reserved for the deal section
