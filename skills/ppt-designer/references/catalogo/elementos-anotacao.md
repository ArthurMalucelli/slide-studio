# Anotação (40)

Marcas que fazem o gráfico argumentar.

Ordem: qualidade, depois deck. ★★★ é a referência a copiar. Copie a técnica, nunca o conteúdo nem as cores do deck de origem.

## ★★★ Bar segments continued into captions
- **Origem:** BTG Pactual, p. 18, 19
- **Recorte:** `crops/e_b3_bar_segments_to_captions.jpg`
- **Peça pronta:** `pecas_elementos_a.py::bar_segments_captions`
- **Função:** Uses the last stacked bar as a table row header: each segment height sets the height of its explanation.
- **Serve para:** Segmented markets where each layer needs a qualitative note.
- **Como montar:** Last column of the stacked bar touches a block of pale rows of equal heights to its segments, tinted to match; each row has a title and a reading; from one row, a thin line and diamond run right to a detail panel, where a curved thick arrow carries 74% between two TAM numbers.
