# Navegação (26)

Trackers, agendas e localizadores.

Ordem: qualidade, depois deck. ★★★ é a referência a copiar. Copie a técnica, nunca o conteúdo nem as cores do deck de origem.

## ★★★ Storyline rail with rotated message
- **Origem:** AB InBev, p. 6, 7, 13, 15, 20, 28, 38, 50
- **Recorte:** `crops/e_b1_ab_rail.jpg`
- **Peça pronta:** `pecas_elementos_a.py::storyline_rail`
- **Função:** Gives each slide a two- or three-word plot point, so the left edge alone tells the story as the reader flips.
- **Serve para:** Long decks with an argument per slide; separating main body from appendix by rail colour alone.
- **Como montar:** Full-height solid brand rail about 6% of width; page number at top in small white; rotated 90-degree large white phrase bottom-aligned ('Evolving Mindsets', 'Dream Big'), not the section name. Rail turns dark grey in the appendix (p79).

## ★★★ Three-level tracker: section, chapter, slide idea

## ★★★ Master plan re-entered as tracker
- **Origem:** CSN (Liability Management), p. 9, 10, 15, 23, 33
- **Recorte:** `crops/e_b2_cs_plan_tracker.jpg`
- **Peça pronta:** `pecas_elementos.py::plan_tracker`
- **Função:** Shows where the advisory plan stands by returning to the same four-step page with only the current step lit.
- **Serve para:** Multi-step plans, transaction sequences, any deck with one governing framework
