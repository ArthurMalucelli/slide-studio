## ★★★ Staircase term tree
- **Origem:** Alupar, p. 22, 23, 24
- **Recorte:** `crops/e_b2_al_stair_tree.jpg`
- **Peça pronta:** `pecas_elementos_b.py::stair_tree`
- **Função:** Lets the reader step through a debt instrument one term at a time.
- **Serve para:** Term sheets, multi-tranche financing
